### Monthly operating rhythm

1. Week one: research and outline; verify intent against the existing article index.
2. Week two: draft, solve every example, and check citations.
3. Week three: edit for clarity, accessibility, mobile layout, metadata, and internal links.
4. Week four: publish, observe Search Console data when available, and record reader questions for the next brief.

If one article per week is not sustainable, publish two strong pieces per month and keep the same sequence. Consistency and usefulness matter more than volume.
